# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Nyaya AI is built for people who are often at their most vulnerable — facing the
police, an abusive home, a leaked photo, a debt they cannot pay. Everyone who
takes part in this project, in any capacity, is expected to behave in a way that
would be acceptable to those users if they were reading.

We welcome contributors regardless of caste, religion, region, language, gender,
gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, appearance, education or
economic background.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people whose first language is not
  English, and with people who are new to programming.
- Give and accept criticism about the work, not about the person.
- Assume good faith. Ask what someone meant before assuming the worst.
- When someone reports a wrong legal answer, thank them. That report may prevent
  real harm.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Slurs or demeaning remarks based on any personal characteristic, including
  caste and religion.
- Publishing someone's private information, or details of someone's legal matter,
  without their clear permission.
- Any sexual content involving a minor, or any attempt to use this project's
  channels to contact a minor privately.
- Deliberately introducing inaccurate legal information, or removing safety
  routing such as the emergency and legal aid helplines.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Privacy of users, specifically

Issues and pull requests are public. **Do not post someone else's case facts,
name, address, phone number, FIR number or screenshots identifying them** — not
even to illustrate a bug. Describe the problem generically, or redact fully.
This applies to your own details too: think before you post.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers through GitHub. If it involves a
security flaw, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Reports will be handled discreetly. The reporter's identity will not be shared
with the person reported without the reporter's consent.

## Consequences

Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may warn, temporarily block or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaches this code. Breaches involving
child safety or the deliberate corruption of legal information will result in an
immediate permanent ban.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages and code comments — and when someone is representing the project
elsewhere.
