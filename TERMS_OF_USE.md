## 3. Accuracy of the legal texts

The app bundles the complete text of 25 Indian Acts, taken from official Government of India sources — almost all from India Code (`indiacode.nic.in`) — with the source URL recorded in each file.

Even so:

- **The law changes.** Acts are amended, sections are struck down by courts, and new laws replace old ones. A bundled copy is a snapshot from the date it was fetched, not a live feed.
- **Some things are already known to shift.** For example, the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961 with effect from 1 April 2026. Both are bundled and labelled, but you must check which applies to your year.
- **Section 66A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 was struck down** by the Supreme Court in *Shreya Singhal v. Union of India* (2015). It still appears in the printed bare Act text. No one can be charged under it.
- State laws, rules, notifications, and case law are **not** included. Rent control, land, police rules and much else are State subjects and vary widely.

Always verify a section against the current official text before acting on it.
