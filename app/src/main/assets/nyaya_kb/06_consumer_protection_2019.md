# Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Source: Department of Consumer Affairs (consumeraffairs.nic.in). Replaced the 1986 Act; in force from July 2020.

## Where to complain (pecuniary jurisdiction, as amended 2021)
- **District Commission** — claims up to **₹50 lakh**
- **State Commission** — ₹50 lakh to ₹2 crore
- **National Commission (NCDRC)** — above ₹2 crore
- Complaint can be filed where the **complainant resides or works** (not only where the seller is).
- **E-filing**: edaakhil.nic.in — online consumer complaint filing; video-conference hearings allowed.
- Limitation: complaint within **2 years** of cause of action.
- Court fee: nil up to ₹5 lakh claims.
- **National Consumer Helpline: 1915** (or app) for pre-litigation mediation with companies.

## Key rights & concepts
- Six consumer rights: safety; information; choice; being heard; redressal; consumer education.
- **Product liability** (Chapter VI): manufacturer/seller/service provider liable for harm from defective products — no need to prove negligence for manufacturing defects.
- **Unfair trade practices** and **unfair contracts** actionable; misleading advertisements punishable — **CCPA** (Central Consumer Protection Authority) can fine manufacturers/endorsers up to ₹10 lakh (₹50 lakh repeat) and ban endorsers.
- **E-commerce** platforms covered (Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020): must disclose seller details, return/refund policy, grievance officer (48h acknowledgement, 1 month resolution).
- **Mediation** as alternate resolution (Chapter V); no appeal against consent decree.
- Appeals: District → State (45 days), State → National, National → Supreme Court.
