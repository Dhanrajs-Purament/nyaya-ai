# Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI)

Act No. 22 of 2005. Source: India Code / rti.gov.in. Empowers any **citizen** to obtain information from public authorities.

## How to file an RTI application (Section 6)
1. Write an application (any format; some states have forms) to the **Public Information Officer (PIO)** of the department.
2. Fee: **₹10** (Central Government; states vary). **Below Poverty Line applicants pay no fee** (attach BPL proof).
3. File online for Central Government at **rtionline.gov.in**.
4. No need to give reasons for seeking information (Section 6(2)).

## Timelines (Section 7)
- **30 days** for normal replies; **48 hours** where information concerns **life or liberty**; +5 days if filed with an Assistant PIO; if third-party information, 40 days.
- If no reply in time → **deemed refusal**, and information must then be provided free.

## Appeals (Section 19)
- **First appeal**: to a senior officer in the same department within 30 days.
- **Second appeal**: to the **Central/State Information Commission** within 90 days.
- Penalty on PIO for wrongful refusal/delay: **₹250 per day up to ₹25,000** (Section 20).

## Exemptions (Section 8)
National security/sovereignty; forbidden by court; parliamentary privilege; commercial confidence/trade secrets; fiduciary relationship; foreign government confidence; endangers life/safety of informants; investigation impediment; cabinet papers (until decision); **personal information with no public interest**. Note: information that cannot be denied to Parliament cannot be denied to a citizen.
