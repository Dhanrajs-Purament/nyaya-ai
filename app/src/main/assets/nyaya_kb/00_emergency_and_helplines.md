# Emergency Numbers & Legal Helplines (India)

## Emergencies
- **112** — National emergency number (police, fire, ambulance; works across India)
- **100** — Police
- **1091** — Women in distress helpline
- **181** — Women helpline (domestic abuse, harassment)
- **1098** — CHILDLINE (children in danger or distress)
- **14567** — Senior citizens helpline
- **1930** — Cyber financial fraud helpline (report immediately; also file at cybercrime.gov.in)

## Free legal help
- **15100** — NALSA free legal aid helpline (National Legal Services Authority)
- Every District Court has a **District Legal Services Authority (DLSA)** front office where you can ask for a free lawyer.
- Online application: nalsa.gov.in → Apply for Legal Aid.

## If someone is arrested — immediate steps
1. Ask the officers for the **grounds of arrest** (this is a legal right — BNSS Section 47).
2. Ask which **police station** the person is being taken to and note officer names/badge numbers.
3. The arrested person has the right to have a **relative or friend informed** (BNSS Section 48).
4. The person must be produced before a **Magistrate within 24 hours** (BNSS Section 58; Constitution Article 22).
5. The person has the right to **meet a lawyer during interrogation** and to free legal aid if they cannot afford one.
6. A woman cannot be arrested **after sunset and before sunrise** except in exceptional circumstances with a woman officer and prior permission of a Magistrate (BNSS Section 43(5)).
