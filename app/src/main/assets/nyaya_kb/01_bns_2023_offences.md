# Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS) — Offences & Punishments

Act No. 45 of 2023. In force from 1 July 2024. **Replaces the Indian Penal Code, 1860 (IPC).** 20 chapters, 358 sections. Source: India Code (indiacode.nic.in), Ministry of Home Affairs.

## Punishments (Section 4)
Death; imprisonment for life; imprisonment (rigorous/simple); forfeiture of property; fine; **community service** (new in BNS, for petty offences).

## New offences introduced by BNS
- **Section 111 — Organised crime** (including syndicates, contract killing, extortion, cyber-crimes as organised crime).
- **Section 112 — Petty organised crime** (vehicle theft, pickpocketing, cheating gangs).
- **Section 113 — Terrorist act** (now in the general criminal code).
- **Section 69** — Sexual intercourse by deceitful means or false promise of marriage.
- **Section 103(2)** — Murder by a group on grounds of race, caste, community (mob lynching): life imprisonment or death.
- **Community service** as punishment (e.g., petty theft under ₹5,000 on return of property, defamation, misconduct by drunken person).

## Structure of BNS (chapters)
I Preliminary (1–3); II Punishments (4–13); III General Exceptions incl. right of private defence (14–44); IV Abetment, conspiracy, attempt (45–62); **V Offences against woman and child (63–99)**; **VI Offences affecting human body (100–146)**; VII Offences against the State (147–158); VIII Army/Navy/Air Force (159–168); IX Elections (169–177); X Coin, currency, stamps (178–188); XI Public tranquillity (189–197); XII Public servants (198–205); XIII Contempt of lawful authority (206–226); XIV False evidence & offences against public justice (227–269); XV Public health, safety, morals (270–297); XVI Religion (298–302); **XVII Offences against property (303–334)**; XVIII Documents & property marks incl. forgery (335–350); XIX Criminal intimidation, insult, annoyance, defamation (351–357); XX Repeal & savings (358).

## General exceptions (defences) — Sections 14–44
Mistake of fact (14, 17); judicial acts (15–16); accident (18); necessity (19); child under 7 (20); child 7–12 of immature understanding (21); unsoundness of mind (22); involuntary intoxication (23); consent (25–30); duress/threats (32); trifling harm (33). **Right of private defence: Sections 34–44** — defence of body/property; extends to causing death when there is reasonable apprehension of death, grievous hurt, rape, kidnapping, acid attack (38) or in defined property offences (41).
