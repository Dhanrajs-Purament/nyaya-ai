# Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)

Act No. 22 of 2023. Source: MeitY (meity.gov.in). India's data privacy law for digital personal data.

## Data principal rights (the individual)
- Right to **access** a summary of personal data processed and processing activities.
- Right to **correction, completion, updating and erasure** of personal data.
- Right to **grievance redressal** with the data fiduciary (company), then complaint to the **Data Protection Board of India**.
- Right to **nominate** a person to exercise rights in case of death or incapacity.

## Obligations of data fiduciaries (companies/apps)
- Process personal data only with **consent** (free, specific, informed, unconditional, unambiguous, by clear affirmative action) or for defined **legitimate uses**.
- Consent request must be in **clear, plain language** (available in English + 22 scheduled languages) with a contact for grievances; **consent can be withdrawn** as easily as it was given.
- Data minimisation, accuracy, security safeguards, **breach notification** to the Board and affected users.
- **Children (<18)**: verifiable parental consent required; no tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted ads at children.
- Significant Data Fiduciaries: data protection officer in India, audits, impact assessments.

## Penalties
Up to **₹250 crore** per instance for failure of security safeguards; ₹200 crore for children-related and breach-notification violations; imposed by the Data Protection Board.

## Exemptions
State security/public order, research/statistics, legal claims, courts, startups (as notified), personal/domestic use, publicly available data made public by the individual.
